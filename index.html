01 / RESEARCH

From physical questions to measurable signals

At the intersection
of light and matter.

My work explores electron acceleration, magnetic-field generation, and shock-wave dynamics in laser-produced plasmas. I combine experiments, diagnostics, and particle-in-cell modelling to study these interactions.

LASER → PLASMA → PARTICLESCONCEPTUAL VIEW
An illustrative interaction, not experimental data.
01 Electron acceleration −

Direct laser acceleration in longitudinal and transverse electric fields, including a practical multi-beam acceleration scheme studied with two-dimensional SMILEI simulations on the TIFR HPC cluster.

Read the preprint ↗
02 High-intensity experiments +

Experiments at the 1 PW laser facility at ELI-NP, Romania, investigating directional MeV gamma-ray emission alongside electron, proton, and neutron emission.

Additional contributions to experimental chambers and diagnostics for the new petawatt facility in Hyderabad.

03 Fields, shocks & diagnostics +

Shock-wave studies with high-contrast 400 nm pulses; laser absorption under varying pulse energy, incidence angle, and prepulse conditions.

Stokes polarimetry for megagauss magnetic fields, pump–probe diagnostics, Doppler-shift measurements, and wavefront sensing.

I came to laser–plasma physics through mechanical engineering. Today, I’m pursuing an integrated PhD in the Department of Atomic and Nuclear Physics at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research.

My experience brings together numerical models, experimental setups, and diagnostic systems. I’m interested in the conversation between them: what a model predicts, what an instrument can resolve, and what an experiment reveals.
